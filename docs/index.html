🦅 Eagles Wire

        Push alerts

        Alerts come through the free ntfy app:

        1. Install ntfy from the App Store.
        2. Open it, tap +, and type the topic name your parent gave you.
        3. Allow notifications when it asks.

        You'll get: every real Eagles story (moves, injuries, coaching), game day reminders, kickoff, every Eagles score, halftime and the final, plus the biggest news from around the league.

        Add to Home Screen

        In Safari tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. Eagles Wire then opens full screen like a regular app.
