    My team

    Your team's stories get highlighted and show up first in the Briefing.

    Push alerts

    Alerts come through the free ntfy app:

    1. Install ntfy from the App Store.
    2. Open it, tap +, and type the topic name your parent gave you.
    3. Allow notifications when it asks.

    Add to Home Screen

    In Safari tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. NFL Wire then opens full screen like a regular app.
